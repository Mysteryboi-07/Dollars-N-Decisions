# Dollars-N-Decisions
Y3.1 Capstone Gamification Simulator Project

## Background Content

Dollars N Decisions is a financial literacy simulation game designed to help teenagers develop foundational financial decision-making skills through an interactive working-adult scenario.

Players experience a simulated one-month financial cycle where they earn income, manage daily expenses, maintain their hunger and happiness, work towards a savings goal and pay their monthly rent.

The game combines financial literacy with simulation and gamification, allowing players to experience the consequences of financial decisions in a safe environment without real-world financial loss.

## Project Objectives

- Provide teenagers with a safe environment to practise financial decision-making.
- Introduce foundational financial concepts such as budgeting, saving and managing expenses.
- Encourage players to consider the relationship between income, spending and financial goals.
- Demonstrate how personal wellbeing can influence financial decision-making.
- Make financial literacy more engaging through simulation and game mechanics.

## Game Objectives

The player's main objective is to successfully manage their finances throughout the one-month game cycle while reaching their savings goal.

Players must:

- Earn money through work activities.
- Manage daily spending.
- Purchase food to maintain hunger.
- Maintain their happiness.
- Pay their monthly rent.
- Reach their savings goal.
- Make decisions about optional financial risks such as gambling.
- Avoid running out of money.

Players can also choose to increase their monthly rent at the beginning of the game as an additional challenge.

## Target Audience

**Teenagers aged 13–19**

The game focuses on foundational financial literacy rather than advanced financial concepts such as investments, taxes and insurance. This makes the financial situations more relatable and approachable for younger players.

## Key Mechanics

### Work & Income
Players complete work-related minigames to earn money.

### Hunger
Hunger decreases after actions such as working, sleeping and gambling.

If hunger remains below 25% for more than two consecutive days, the player receives a Game Over.

Low hunger can also affect work efficiency, giving the player a 60% chance for a work activity to take two phases instead of one.

### Happiness
Happiness changes based on the player's actions.

- Working decreases happiness.
- Sleeping increases happiness.
- Gambling increases happiness.

Lower happiness can reduce the amount of money earned from work, encouraging players to balance income with their wellbeing.

### Spending
Players purchase food from the supermarket to maintain their hunger while managing their available money and savings goal.

Food is organised into categories such as:

- Cereals
- Snacks
- Cup noodles
- Fruits
- Drinks

### Monthly Rent
Players are required to pay rent as a monthly financial commitment.

The player can voluntarily increase their rent at the beginning of the game to create a greater financial challenge.

### Gambling
Players can access a gambling feature through their work computer.

This introduces an optional risk-reward decision where players can potentially gain money but also risk losing it.

## Controls

**W** – Move Forward  
**A** – Move Left  
**S** – Move Backward  
**D** – Move Right  

**Mouse** – Look Around / Interact with UI

## Game Flow

1. Start the game and complete the tutorial.
2. Set your savings goal.
3. Select your monthly rent.
4. Begin the daily routine.
5. Complete work activities to earn money.
6. Purchase food when necessary.
7. Manage hunger and happiness.
8. Decide whether to take financial risks such as gambling.
9. Continue managing your finances throughout the month.
10. Pay your monthly rent and work towards your savings goal.
11. Complete the month while maintaining your financial and wellbeing requirements.

## Limitations / Known Bugs

### Supermarket
- Food is currently grouped into broader categories rather than individual products.
- Players cannot compare different products within the same category.


### Environment
- Some assets may have minor overlapping.
- Environmental detail and lighting are limited due to project performance and development time.

### Gameplay
- The game currently follows a fixed one-month cycle, limiting replayability.
- Only one fixed financial scenarios is available.
- The game does not currently include a recurring mascot character.

## Future Improvements

- Add multiple financial scenarios and difficulty levels.
- Expand the supermarket into a more detailed product purchasing and comparison system.
- Add more varied financial events and unexpected expenses.
- Introduce a recurring mascot to strengthen the game's identity.
- Improve environmental details and lighting.
- Add more visual feedback for player financial decisions.
- Expand the game to provide greater replayability.
